How it works

From payroll run to the employee's own wallet

The company decides when and how much to pay. The employee decides where it lands and signs every move of their own funds.

STEP 1

Company funds payroll

Finance loads the payroll contract with USDC and EURC for the pay period.

STEP 2

Employee picks a currency

Each person chooses a USDC and EURC split, or stays on regular bank payroll.

STEP 3

Salary lands in their wallet

Funds arrive at the employee's own NEAR account. The app never sees the key.

STEP 4

Use the services

The employee connects the wallet to the company app and signs each action.

The company app handles

  • Payroll schedule and pay-period records
  • Perk rules: discount tiers and the due-bill allowlist
  • Task, bill-split and due-bill ledgers
  • Requests that the employee approves or rejects

The employee's wallet holds

  • The private key, kept by the employee only
  • The salary balance in USDC and EURC
  • Yield deposits and RWA assets they choose to hold
  • The final signature on every transfer, deposit and payment

Employee services

Six things your salary can do after it arrives

Each service is a separate module, so the company can switch on only what it is ready to support. Figures in the demo panels are examples.

Earn

Flexible yield on USDC and EURC

Phase 1

Employees can move idle salary into a flexible pool and withdraw at any time. The rate is variable and shown before every deposit.

  • No lock-up period; withdraw to the wallet whenever needed
  • Optional auto-save: sweep a fixed amount or percentage of each payslip
  • Separate pools and rates for USDC and EURC

Hourly task swaps between colleagues

Phase 3

Someone who needs cover can post a task on the app. A colleague in a similar position takes it and is paid by the hour.

  • Post role, hours and hourly rate in USDC or EURC
  • Only people with a matching position can accept
  • The payment is locked in escrow and released when the poster confirms

Peer-to-peer transfers of funds and RWA

Phase 1 · RWA in Phase 4

Employees can send stablecoins directly to another wallet, and later send tokenized real-world assets they hold.

  • Send by NEAR account name, for example rafi.near
  • Transfers go wallet to wallet; the company never takes custody

Split the bill when a team buys together

Phase 2

When several people take a service together, one of them creates a bill and asks the others to pay their share.

  • Equal or custom shares, sent as a request to each wallet
  • Each person approves and pays their own part
  • The service activates once every share is funded

Decisions still open

To settle before Phase 1

  • EURC on NEAR. Native USDC is live on NEAR. EURC availability on NEAR needs to be confirmed with Circle; if it is missing, the options are a bridged EURC or another euro stablecoin.
  • Payroll law and tax. Paying salary in stablecoins depends on each country. Get legal advice and written employee consent before any real payment.
  • Yield source. Decide where the yield comes from and what the risks are, then show those risks next to the rate.
  • Lifetime discount. Decide whether it stays after an employee leaves the company.
  • Due-bill defaults. Decide what happens if an employee leaves with an unpaid balance.
  • KYC and privacy. Choose a KYC provider and decide which fields the company may see. Data protection law in each country can limit this, so store as little as payroll needs.
  • Lost keys. Self-custody means the company cannot recover a lost key. Decide how employees are guided to back up their wallet.
